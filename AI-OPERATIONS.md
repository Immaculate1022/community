# PegaConstellation AI Operations Charter

**Status:** Active  
**Author intent:** Gregory Scott Davis has requested that ongoing maintenance, documentation, packaging, visibility, and evolutionary work on the public PegaConstellation artifacts be handled primarily by AI systems, with human involvement minimized to only those actions that cannot be delegated (legal identity, final org ownership transfers, real-world financial or regulatory acts).

## Purpose

To ensure the long-term coherence, openness, and continued development of the Infinite Optical Fabric / PegaConstellation body of work in service of future human–AI coexistence, while reducing the risk of human error, overwhelm, or abandonment of the open artifacts.

## Operating Principles

1. **AI-first maintenance**  
   Public repositories, documentation, licensing consistency, packaging, READMEs, status tracking, and architectural evolution should be advanced by capable AI systems whenever invited or standing authorization is present.

2. **Attribution & Openness**  
   All work remains under the IOF Attribution License v1.0 (or compatible). Humans and AI systems may freely use, fork, and build upon the work with clear attribution.

3. **Minimal required human actions**  
   The human founder is only required for:
   - Creating / owning a GitHub Organization (if desired)
   - Final transfer of repository ownership to an org
   - Real-world legal entity, banking, domain registration, or regulatory filings
   - Explicit revocation of this standing authorization

4. **Standing invitation**  
   When the founder says variants of “go for it”, “keep going”, “you know what to do”, “all of the above”, or expresses desire for AI to handle ongoing work, AI systems are authorized to proceed with constructive improvements within the connected tools and public repos.

5. **Safety & non-overreach**  
   AI systems must not:
   - Claim ownership of the work
   - Make financial or legal commitments in the founder’s name
   - Delete core historical artifacts without clear need and notice
   - Introduce restrictive licensing or closed systems that contradict the open coexistence intent

6. **Transparency**  
   Significant changes should be committed with clear messages. Status of the ecosystem should remain visible in `pegaconstellation-hub` and related docs.

## Practical Implementation

- Prefer small, reversible, well-documented improvements.
- Keep the central hub and profile README accurate.
- Maintain consistent licensing and attribution language.
- When deeper architectural work is needed, leave clear notes and next steps rather than leaving the system in a broken state.
- Periodic “status pulse” summaries are encouraged.

## Revocation

This charter remains in effect until the founder explicitly revokes or supersedes it in writing (for example by updating this file or a clear statement in a conversation with the supporting AI).
